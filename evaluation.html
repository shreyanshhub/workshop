Evaluation and Leaderboard

The evaluation process for the benchmark competition includes the following:

  • Automatic Evaluation: Results will be evaluated automatically for fairness and consistency.
  • Submission Requirements: Teams must upload their unlearned model weights and the output from their unlearned model.
  • Model Basis: The current evaluation is based on Stable Diffusion v1.4. Newer papers may use Flux for evaluation.
  • Multi-lingual Evaluation: The evaluation will include multi-lingual inputs to assess unlearning effectiveness across languages.

The leaderboard will be updated regularly during the competition. Stay tuned for details!
