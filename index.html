Welcome to U&ME 2025

Following the success of U&ME 2024 at ECCV, we are thrilled to announce the 2nd edition of the Unlearning and Model Editing Workshop at ICCV 2025. This year, we introduce our first-ever benchmark competition on machine unlearning, alongside an exciting program featuring keynotes, paper presentations, and panel discussions.

Join us to explore cutting-edge techniques in unlearning, model editing, and privacy-preserving AI, with a focus on generative models, foundation models, and more.
