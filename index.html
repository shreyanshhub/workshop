Welcome to U&ME 2025

Building on the success of U&ME 2025 at ECCV, we are excited to host the 2nd Unlearning and Model Editing Workshop at ICCV 2025. This year, we spotlight generative machine unlearning, a critical area in privacy-preserving AI. Generative models, such as those used in image synthesis and text generation, are driving innovation but also raise significant privacy concerns. These models often memorize training data, making it challenging to comply with regulations like the EU’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, which enforce the "right to be forgotten."

Generative machine unlearning seeks to erase the influence of specific training data from these models without compromising their performance. This emerging field explores new theoretical frameworks, practical algorithms, and scalable solutions to ensure ethical AI practices. Recent works, including surveys by Zhang et al. (2023), Nguyen et al. (2022), and Jiang et al. (2022), highlight the growing importance of this domain. At U&ME 2025, we’ll feature keynotes, paper presentations, and a benchmark competition to advance research in this space.

Generative Machine Unlearning Competition

Our competition aims to standardize evaluation metrics for generative unlearning, enabling fair comparisons of algorithms and uncovering their strengths and limitations. By welcoming participants globally, we aim to inspire innovative solutions and tackle open challenges. Hosted on Kaggle from mid-August 2023 to mid-November 2023, the competition is currently closed.
