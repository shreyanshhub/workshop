About the Workshop

The Unlearning and Model Editing (U&ME) Workshop 2025 aims to bring together researchers, practitioners, and policymakers to address the challenges of machine unlearning and model editing in AI. With the growing need for privacy-preserving technologies, this workshop focuses on techniques to remove specific data from trained models while maintaining performance, as well as editing models to correct biases or update knowledge.

Building on the success of U&ME 2024 at ECCV, this year’s workshop at ICCV 2025 introduces a benchmark competition to standardize evaluation in generative unlearning, alongside keynotes, paper presentations, and discussions on topics like foundation models, generative AI, and ethical AI.
