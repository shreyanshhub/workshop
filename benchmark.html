Benchmark Competition

The U&ME Workshop 2025 introduces its first-ever benchmark competition to standardize the field of generative unlearning. The benchmark aims to:

  • Taxonomy: Standardize the terms and consolidate the field.
  • Evaluation Process: Address the lack of a standard evaluation framework in generative unlearning.

Timeline and Prizes

Details on the competition timeline and prizes will be announced soon. Stay tuned for updates!
